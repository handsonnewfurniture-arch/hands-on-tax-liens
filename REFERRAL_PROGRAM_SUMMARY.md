## 💰 Economics (Why This is Brilliant)

### Current Paid Ads CAC
- Cost: **$150 per customer**
- Channel: Google Ads, Facebook

### Referral Program CAC
- **Hard Cost: $52 per customer** (65% savings!)
  - Referee discount: $50
  - Infrastructure: $2
  - Cash payout: **$0** ✅

- **Opportunity Cost: $65** (free month to referrer)
  - But this is just foregone revenue, not cash out
  - Actually increases referrer LTV and retention

### Why Free Months Beat $25 Cash

**Cash Model Problems:**
- $25 real expense
- Payment processing fees
- Tax complexity (1099s)
- Total cost: ~$27

**Free Month Model Advantages:**
- $0 hard cost (92% cheaper)
- Higher perceived value ($29-$199 vs $25)
- Increases referrer retention
- Encourages tier upgrades
- Creates "stickier" customers

## 🔒 Fraud Prevention

1. **Email verification** required for referee
2. **Payment required** for conversion (no free trials)
3. **30-day retention** requirement before referrer gets reward
4. **IP/device fingerprinting** to prevent self-referrals
5. **Manual review** for suspicious patterns (>10 refs/day)
6. **Max 1 referral per email address**
